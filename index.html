Privacy Policy for AI Writing Layer

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 • Version 1.0 • Compliant with Google Play & DPDP Act 2023

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Writing Layer ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you install and use our Android Input Method Editor (IME) and AI writing assistant. We are committed to maintaining the highest level of user privacy and data security.

2. Zero Keystroke Logging Policy

We do not log, capture, or store your day-to-day keystrokes. All regular keyboard interactions (typing letters, numbers, symbols, spaces, backspaces, and punctuation) are processed entirely within the local memory of your Android device. Your typing data is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties during normal keyboard operation.

3. On-Demand AI Processing

When you explicitly activate an AI feature by tapping the floating AI orb or an action button (such as Smart Reply, Translate, Improve, or Shorten):

  • Selected Text Snippet: Only the specific message or text snippet you have selected or pasted is transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS to our AI processing endpoint.
  • App Context: The package name of the active app (e.g. com.whatsapp or org.telegram.messenger) is optionally sent as a formatting hint so replies match chat or email standards. No app contents or user identifiers from that app are retrieved.
  • Installation Identifier: A randomly generated pseudonymous installation ID is included in request headers solely for rate limiting and preventing denial-of-service abuse.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Handling: Text sent for AI generation is processed in volatile server memory solely for the purpose of generating candidates. It is never used to train foundational AI models and is not persistently retained.

4. Automatic Password & Sensitive Field Shield

In strict accordance with Google Play policies, our keyboard automatically detects password fields (InputType.TYPE_TEXT_VARIATION_PASSWORD, TYPE_TEXT_VARIATION_WEB_PASSWORD, etc.) and disables all AI suggestions, clipboard context, and floating actions. No data from password or PIN fields is ever processed by AI.

5. Microphone & Audio Usage

The android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO permission is requested exclusively when you initiate voice typing. Audio is converted to text in real time using on-device or standard speech recognition services. We do not store or transmit raw audio recordings to any persistent database.

6. Data Retention & Erasure

Because we do not maintain user accounts or personal profiles, no persistent database of your personal typing history exists on our servers. You may clear all local preferences anytime via Android Settings → Apps → AI Writing Layer → Clear Data.

7. Grievance Redressal & Contact

For privacy inquiries, rights requests under the DPDP Act 2023, or policy questions, please contact our Data Protection Officer at:
Email: privacy@aiwritinglayer.app

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance

Compliance Statement for Indian Users & Global Standards

1. Overview of DPDP Alignment

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) mandates that Data Fiduciaries handle digital personal data lawfully, transparently, and strictly for specified purposes. AI Writing Layer operates with privacy by design:

DPDP Principle AI Writing Layer Implementation
Purpose Limitation Text submitted via AI actions is processed strictly to generate immediate writing suggestions and discarded.
Data Minimisation Day-to-day keystrokes remain 100% on-device. No extraneous personal data or contacts are collected.
Storage Limitation Zero persistent storage of user text snippets. Processing occurs in ephemeral server RAM.
Security Safeguards Encrypted TLS 1.3 in-transit communications, R8 ProGuard code obfuscation, and password auto-shielding.

2. Rights of the Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you as a Data Principal hold specific statutory rights:

  • Right to Information: The right to know what personal data is processed, which is detailed in this document.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Because we do not store persistent logs or account profiles, there is no personal data record stored to erase. Uninstalling the app removes all local tokens.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Any concerns may be directly reported to our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke keyboard activation or microphone permission at any time in Android settings.
